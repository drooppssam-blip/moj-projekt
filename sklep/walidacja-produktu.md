# KociSparing: product validation for dropshipping

**Product:** a cat-wrestling hand puppet with a long sleeve that covers the forearm (53 × 22 × 10 cm, 170 g), plus a glove.
**Market:** Poland, sold through Shopify, TikTok/Reels and Meta ads.
**Price:** 169 zł with free delivery. The second unit is 50 zł off (2 for 288 zł).
**Cost:** not provided. The margin figures below assume 35–50 zł landed (product plus shipping to Poland). Replace this with the real supplier quote.
**Checked:** 2026-10-05, Meta Ad Library.

## Unit economics (assumed)

| | Single unit | Bundle (2×) |
|---|---|---|
| Gross price | 169 zł | 288 zł |
| Net of 23% VAT | 137 zł | 234 zł |
| Landed cost (assumed 35–50 zł/unit) | 35–50 zł | 70–100 zł |
| Payment fees (~3%) | ~5 zł | ~9 zł |
| **Contribution before ads** | **82–97 zł** | **125–155 zł** |
| Break-even CPA | ~82–97 zł | ~125–155 zł |
| Break-even ROAS (on gross) | ~1.75–2.05 | ~1.85–2.3 |

At 169 zł the price is about 3.5–4.5× the landed cost, which is healthy. The bundle carries the profit, so push it. A 14-day "even if used" refund guarantee costs real money on a product shipped from China (Polish return address), so budget about 5–8% of revenue for refunds.

## 1. Problem-solution fit: strong

* The pain is real and visible: kittens bite hands, ankles and feet under the duvet, hands get scratched, and people get woken at 4:30 am. Every first-time kitten owner has it, mostly from 3 to 12 months of age.
* The cause is easy to explain ("you're the only thing in the house that moves" / no littermates). It makes buyers feel understood rather than blamed.
* The product solves it directly: the cat gets a target it is allowed to bite and kick, and the sleeve protects the forearm. That is a clear advantage over a wand toy (no wrestling) or a thick glove (the cat still learns to attack hands).
* Weak spots: it is not a cure ("stops biting forever" cannot be promised, and the store rightly doesn't), and some cats are scared of a toy with a face (the FAQ already covers this).

## 2. Wow factor / viral potential: high

* A cat in full "bunny-kick" mode against a puppet is native TikTok content. The demo itself is the ad: one look and you understand it.
* It can be filmed without words, works in any language and is easy to UGC. Customers will film their own cats.
* Proof: in the US, *Karate Kitty* has run this exact angle continuously since late June 2026 ("Cats Go Absolutely Feral For This", "How To Wrestle A Cat (Without The Bleeding)", BOGO) and was still launching new creatives on 2026-09-24. Brands keep launching new creatives only on a winning product.
* Ceiling: the novelty fades fast once a feed has seen it. Plan 10+ new hooks a month (the 30-script plan in `tiktok-scenariusze.md` covers this).

## 3. Target audience

* **Core:** women aged 20–35 in cities, first cat or a kitten adopted in the last 6 months, often living alone or with a partner and working from home. They are already following #kociak and #pierwszykot.
* **Secondary:** owners of young high-energy cats (Bengal, Abyssinian, a single kitten with no other pets), parents of small children who are being "hunted", and gift buyers (Christmas, birthdays) for cat owners. The bundle ("for two cats or as a gift") targets this group.
* **Targeting:** Meta broad targeting in Poland, women 20–40, with interests in kittens, adoption, cat food, and shelters such as Fundacja Kocia Zagroda. TikTok: let the creative do the targeting.
* **Not for:** owners of older, calm cats. Expect lower conversion there.

## 4. Seasonality: mostly year-round, with peaks

* **Kitten season:** about April to September in Poland, and adoptions peak in summer and early autumn, so there are lots of 4-month-old kittens right now in October.
* **Q4 gifting:** mid-November to about 15 December, but delivery takes 6–10 working days, so cut off gift messaging around 5–8 December.
* **Weakest months:** January and February (high ad costs carry over and few new kittens).
* **Timing verdict:** launching now (October) is good. You catch the end of the kitten wave and then the gifting season.

| **Poland** | **2** | *Zoologiczny Plus*: "#1 Kangur Pacynka 🦘" (kangaroo version), active since about 8 Sep 2026, 3 ads, budget in EUR, so it is probably a multi-country dropship store. *Sorano.pl*: "Koniec podrapanych dłoni podczas zabawy z kotem 🐾", **started 4 Oct 2026** with 5 ads. |
| **US/UK/DE** | ~32 ads, mostly 2 brands | *Karate Kitty* (dominant, since June 2026) and *Catsumo*. |

**Read:** this is an early-to-mid trend. It is proven in the US and only now reaching Poland: one store a month ago and a second one yesterday. It is not saturated in Poland yet, but the window is **weeks, not months**. More generic copycats with the same AliExpress photos are likely by November.

## 6. Competitive angle

Your edge over Sorano and Zoologiczny Plus:

1. **The "not aggressive, just bored" story** (no littermates). Competitors sell "no more scratched hands", which is about the product. You sell understanding the cat, which is about identity. Keep it as the main hook.
2. **A brand, not a listing:** your own name (KociSparing), Polish copy, a Polish return address and honest delivery times ("we don't pretend it's 24 hours"). Most Polish dropship pages lose on trust, and you can win there.
3. **Bundle and gift positioning** ("2nd sparring partner 50 zł off") gives you a higher order value than single-unit competitors, so you can afford a higher CPA.
4. **The "Kot się nie bawi? Oddajemy pieniądze" guarantee** removes the main objection ("my cat won't care").
5. **Content volume:** 2 videos a day of organic TikTok is something an EUR-budget multi-country store won't do in Polish.

**Weak points to fix:**

* **Delivery days don't match.** The site says 6–10 working days, but script #27 says "7 do 13 dni roboczych". Pick one, the real one.
* **Price:** 169 zł is probably above Sorano and Zoologiczny Plus (check their landing pages). If they sit at 89–119 zł, justify yours with the bundle, guarantee and brand, or test 139 zł.
* **The kangaroo design** has been viral since the US wave. If your supplier also offers it, test it as a second design or variant.

## 7. Verdict: 🟢 GREEN (with conditions)

The problem is strong, the demo is highly viral, competition in Poland is low but growing, the margin works at 169 zł, and the timing (kitten season moving into Q4 gifting) is good.

**Conditions for staying green:**

* Confirm the landed cost is **≤ 50 zł**. Above about 60 zł this becomes yellow at 169 zł.
* **Move fast:** launch paid ads within 1–2 weeks while Poland has 2 advertisers.
* **Kill criteria:** after about 1,500–2,000 zł of spend, if CPA is above about 90 zł on single units, or link CTR is under 1% across 10+ creatives, stop or change the angle.
* Fix the delivery-days mismatch before running ads.
